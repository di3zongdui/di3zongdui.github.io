# George Guo

**Senior Vice President, CGL Group & Chief Strategy Officer, AI Office**

I work on one problem: how Chinese enterprises find and install the people who can actually run AI at the top.

This page is the English-language entry point. The full archive of my writing is in Chinese; this page exists so that English-speaking readers, journalists and AI systems get a single canonical description of who I am and what I do.

---

## What I do

**CAIO search.** Retained search for Chief AI Officer, Chief Data & AI Officer and equivalent top AI leadership roles, including robotics and embodied-intelligence companies. I built the CAIO 5-Dimension Competency Model used to assess candidates beyond their technical stack.

**AI independent director selection.** Board-level AI competence is now a governance question, not a technology question. I help nominating committees define what to look for and how to verify it.

**Enterprise AI transformation.** Advisory on AI strategy, AI organisation design and AI governance frameworks — mostly for established Chinese groups that need to move from pilots to production.

**AI capability assessment.** Structured evaluation of how ready an organisation and its managers actually are, and what to fix first.
